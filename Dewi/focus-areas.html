Our Focus Areas

Five connected areas of work support children, young people, families and communities.

  1. Home
  2. Focus Areas

Child Protection

Promoting the safety, well-being, and rights of vulnerable children through awareness, advocacy, mentorship, and community engagement.

Education Support

Supporting access to learning materials, school mentorship, educational guidance, and literacy development for children facing social and economic challenges.

Youth and Community Empowerment

Encouraging leadership, life skills, positive values, and active participation among young people and local communities.

Peacebuilding and Social Cohesion

Promoting peaceful coexistence, unity, inclusion, and resilience within communities through dialogue and awareness initiatives.

Community Outreach and Humanitarian Support

Engaging communities through outreach activities aimed at improving hope, wellbeing, and support for vulnerable families and children.
